# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML and CSS, one self-contained file per page in `bike-shop/`. Fonts from Google Fonts; no framework, no build step, no external images.

## Users

Online bike buyers on desktop and mobile, in three overlapping groups:

- Riders upgrading: they already ride, know roughly which category they want, and compare specs and prices between models.
- New or returning riders: unsure which bike suits them and looking for guidance before they commit (the bike finder exists for them).
- Commuters: want a dependable city or e-bike for daily travel and care about range, practicality and delivery.

All three compare bikes side by side and hunt for deals. Many arrive on a phone from a deal or a search result and decide in short sessions.

## Product Purpose

Gearline Cycles is an e-commerce bike shop selling road, gravel, city and e-bikes. It exists to get each visitor to the right bike at a fair price with as few steps as possible between finding a bike and putting it in the cart. Success is a visitor who understands the price and the guarantees at a glance and reaches the cart without hunting.

## Positioning

Online only, built for you: every bike is expert-built by mechanics, assembled for free and delivered ready to ride in 48 hours, with a 30-day test ride. The shop competes on that service promise as much as on price.

## Operating Context

- Visitors compare several bikes, often across categories, and return to the deals list repeatedly.
- The quick bike finder ("Find my bike") is the guided path for visitors who do not know what they need.
- The standard trust points are free delivery over €500, 30-day returns, a 2-year warranty, and a 4.8/5 rating from 2,300 reviews.
- Purchases happen on desktop and mobile; both are first-class.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Catalogue: road, gravel, city and e-bikes, each with sizes or a key spec (for example range for e-bikes), price, previous price when discounted, and stock status.
- Deals show the current price, the old price, the discount and real stock counts.
- Undecided: checkout flow, account features, payment options, and the bike finder's full question set.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Gearline Cycles.
- Voice: energetic and sporty, but trustworthy. Confident and direct, never hype. Plain words, short sentences, numbers stated exactly.
- No fake urgency: no countdown timers or invented scarcity. Stock counts must be true.

## Evidence on Hand

All current content is fictional demo material: the brand, product names, prices, discounts, stock counts, policies and the 4.8/5 from 2,300 reviews rating. There are no real photos yet; pages use labelled image placeholders (for example "Product photo: gravel bike, side view"). Do not invent further testimonials, customer names, press quotes, awards or statistics.

## Product Principles

1. Price first: the current price, the old price and the discount are readable in one glance on every product.
2. Trust is visible: delivery, returns, warranty and rating sit near the decision, not in a footer.
3. Short path to cart: every product view offers Add to cart directly; no step that does not help the decision.
4. Compare with ease: products of a kind show the same facts in the same order so they can be compared side by side.
5. Guide the unsure: the bike finder is always one step away for visitors who do not know what to pick.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Stock and status never rely on colour alone (always a dot plus words). Text contrast at least 4.5:1, touch targets at least 44px, working keyboard focus on every control.
